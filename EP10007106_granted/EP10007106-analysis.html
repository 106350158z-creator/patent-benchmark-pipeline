The closest prior art is therefore considered to be a general purpose computer from which the subject-matter of claim 1 differs through the business method running on that computer system. Features which cannot be seen to make any contribution, either independently or in combination with other features, to the solution of a technical problem are not relevant for assessing inventive step. The business method described above is therefore not relevant for assessing inventive step. The person skilled in the art of data processing would consider the implementation of non-technical methods in a computer system to be part of his daily work and therefore would consider it obvious to attempt to implement the business method above in a general purpose networked computer system when presented with the method, for example, in the form of a requirements specification requesting the automation of the method. The presented alleged technical effects in paragraph [ooo4] "through the use of a meta data layer and a data layer according to the resent application, query ... engine is made much faster and multidimensional querying is also made much faster" are not derivable from the claims. D2 mentions a similar problem, so if this problem is derivable from the claim, the solution would be known from D2. For the reasons given above, the examiner is unable to derive any technical problem from the claimed subject-matter. Indeed, the skilled person would translate the non-technical method into a general purpose computer through use of standard data processing techniques as part of his normal daily work without encountering any technical problem. He would therefore arrive at the subject-matter of claim 1 without making an inventive step (Article 56 EPC).
因此，最接近的现有技术被认为是通用计算机，权利要求1的主题与其区别在于运行在该计算机系统上的商业方法。不能被认为单独或组合地对解决技术问题作出贡献的特征，与创造性评价无关。因此，上述商业方法与创造性评价无关。数据处理领域的技术人员会认为将非技术方法实现在计算机系统中属于其日常工作的一部分，因此当以例如要求自动化该方法的需求说明书形式给出该方法时，会认为尝试在通用网络计算机系统中实现上述商业方法是显而易见的。申请人在第[0004]段提出的所谓技术效果，即通过使用元数据层和数据层使查询引擎更快、多维查询也更快，不能从权利要求中导出。D2提到了类似问题，因此如果该问题能从权利要求中导出，其解决方案也会由D2知晓。基于上述理由，审查员不能从权利要求主题中导出任何技术问题。事实上，技术人员会使用标准数据处理技术，将非技术方法转化到通用计算机中，作为其正常日常工作的一部分，且不会遇到任何技术问题。因此，其无需创造性劳动即可得到权利要求1的主题。该段原文直接给出最接近现有技术、区别特征、非技术特征排除、技术效果不可导出以及Article 56显而易见性结论，构成本案创造性核心风险证据；但最终授权决定显示该异议已被克服，故不评为低分而评为80。

The subject matter of claims 1-15 is considered to relate to subject matter excluded ... under Art. 52(2) and (3) EPC. The method defines an abstract idea and is devoid of any technical consideration and therefore, when considered on its own, falls under the list of exclusions from patentability under Article 52(2) and (3) EPC. The technical character of the claim resides in that the data processing steps necessary for the determination of a final model operable to be used to forecast information for an objective are executed by general purpose networked computer having a multidimensional storage. The Board of Appeal finds the data storage system as technical.
权利要求1-15的主题被认为涉及EPC第52(2)和(3)条所排除的主题。该方法定义了一个抽象思想，并且缺乏任何技术考虑，因此当单独考虑时，落入EPC第52(2)和(3)条规定的可专利性排除清单。该权利要求的技术性在于，用于确定可用于预测目标信息的最终模型所必需的数据处理步骤，由具有多维存储的通用网络计算机执行。上诉委员会认为数据存储系统具有技术性。该原文同时显示Article 52适格性异议和技术性承认。按照EPO实践，技术手段使整体具备技术性，非技术内容转入Article 56/COMVIK评价；最终授权表明适格性异议已解决，故评分80。

主要风险

  • Previously found to be a non-technical business method. (曾被认定为非技术商业方法)
  • Inventiveness mainly depends on computer-implementation differences. (创造性主要依赖计算机实现差异)
  • The query-acceleration effect was previously considered absent from the claims. (查询加速效果曾被认为未写入权利要求)
  • D2 discloses known OLAP multidimensional data processing. (D2披露已知OLAP多维数据处理)

建议动作

  • Emphasize the technical combination of multidimensional data storage, metadata layer, data layer, and aggregation rules in enforcement or licensing materials. (在维权或许可文件中突出授权权利要求中的多维数据存储、元数据层、数据层和聚合规则的技术组合。)
  • Prepare an element-by-element comparison with D2, identifying technical limitations in the granted claims that D2 does not disclose or suggest. (准备一份与D2的逐项对比表，明确授权权利要求中未由D2公开或暗示的技术限定。)
  • Add internal technical evidence quantifying how the metadata layer and data layer affect query speed, storage footprint, or processing efficiency for later invalidity or opposition defense. (补充内部技术证据，量化元数据层和数据层对查询速度、存储空间或处理效率的影响，用于后续无效或异议防御。)
  • Avoid describing the invention externally as a mere business prediction model; describe it as a data-processing system improving data storage and multidimensional querying. (避免在对外解释中将发明描述为单纯商业预测模型，应描述为改进数据存储和多维查询的数据处理系统。)
  • Check validation, translation, and renewal-fee status in each designated state to ensure the granted rights have not lapsed because of national-stage formalities. (核查各指定国生效、翻译和年费状态，确保授权后权利未因国家阶段手续瑕疵失效。)

The method defines an abstract idea and is devoid of any technical consideration and therefore, when considered on its own, falls under the list of exclusions from patentability under Article 52(2) and (3) EPC. The technical character of the claim resides in that the data processing steps necessary for the determination of a final model operable to be used to forecast information for an objective are executed by general purpose networked computer having a multidimensional storage.
该方法定义了一个抽象思想，并且缺乏任何技术考虑，因此当单独考虑时，落入EPC第52(2)和(3)条规定的可专利性排除清单。该权利要求的技术性在于，用于确定可用于预测目标信息的最终模型所必需的数据处理步骤，由具有多维存储的通用网络计算机执行。该证据同时说明排除性风险与权利要求存在技术手段，是适格性评分为80而非100的原因。
