Claim Draft Preview

A compound of Formula (Iacl): R3S (R3s)0-2 (lac-1), or a solvate,or pharmaceutically acceptable salt thereof, wherein: R2 is -(CH2)n-R2s, wherein n is 0, 1, or 2, and R2s is 4- to 8-membered monocyclic heterocycloalkyl optionally substituted with one or more Ci-C6 alkyl, C2-C6 alkenyl, C2-C6 alkynyl, C1-C6 haloalkyl, halo, -CN, -OH, -O(C1-C6 alkyl), -NH2, -NH(C1-C6 alkyl), -N(C1-C6 alkyl)2, or oxo; and each R3s is independently Ci-C6 alkyl, Ci-Co haloalkyl, C3-Cs cycloalkyl, halo, or Cs-Cs heterocycloalkyl is optionally substituted with -O(Ci-C6 alkyl),-N(Ci-C6 alkyl)2, halo, or -CN.
