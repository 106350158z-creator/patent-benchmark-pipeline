Novelty:
Claims 1-15 are novel over WO 2007/022474 (D1),because they are directed to a
compound for use in multiple sclerosis. In contrast,D1 is directed to a composition
comprising a nucleic acid and a quinolone analog of formulas (I)-(V) for use in the
5
treatment of psoriasis.
Inventive Step:
Claims 1-15 alsoinvolve an inventive step in view of WO 2010/113096(D2) and the
publication by Drygin Denis (D3)for the following reasons.
10
According to the written opinion of the IPRP, D2 would represent the most relevant
state of the art.D2would disclose thatcompoundswhich decrease the expressionlevel
of at least one gene involved in the RNA polymerase I pathway are efficient drugs in
the treatmentof multiplesclerosis (MS).
15
Further, according to the written opinion, D3 would provide an alternative anti MS
drug, because D3 would describe analog CX-5461 as a potent and selective inhibitor of
RNA polymerase I.Accordingly,the combination of D2 and D3would makeit obvious
for a skilled person to try CX-5461 for treating MS.
20
It is disagreed with the IPRP that the skilled person would combine the teachings of
D2 andD3to arrive atthepresentinvention.
First of all, when considering D2, the skilled person would have no reason to consult
25D3 for alternative MS drugs, as D3 does not relate to the treatment of MS.
Second,even when consultingD3,the skilled person would not conclude based on D3
that analog CX-5461 would be a potent alternative MS drug, as explained below.
